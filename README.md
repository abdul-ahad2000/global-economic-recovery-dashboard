# Global Economic Recovery Dashboard (2020–2024)

## Project Overview

This project explores how the COVID-19 pandemic and subsequent economic recovery affected major global economies between 2020 and 2024 using Tableau.

The dashboard analyses key macroeconomic indicators to understand economic disruptions, policy responses, recovery patterns and financial market developments across selected countries.

## Key Areas of Analysis

1. **Unemployment Trends:** Examines unemployment spikes during 2020 and the gradual recovery in subsequent years.
2. **Monetary Policy & Interest Rates:** Explores central bank interest rate reductions during the pandemic and subsequent rate increases to address inflation.
3. **GDP Growth & Recovery:** Compares economic contractions in 2020, the rebound in 2021 and growth patterns through 2024.
4. **Inflation vs. Economic Growth:** Compares CPI and inflation trends against annual GDP growth across selected economies.
5. **Business & Consumer Sentiment:** Examines Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI) and consumer confidence indicators.
6. **Financial Markets:** Highlights market declines during the initial pandemic period, subsequent recovery and market volatility.

## Tools & Technologies
